# DESIGN CONCEPTS — i-pro.tech

## PHASE 1: DISCOVERY & ANALYSIS

### Business Context

**Company:** i-pro.tech  
**Domain:** https://i-pro.tech  
**Service:** Implementation of AI agents into sales departments  
**Business Model:** B2B consulting with high-ticket pricing (hundreds of thousands of rubles)

**Core Process:**
1. Audit of sales processes
2. Data analysis from client's CRM
3. AI agent training on company-specific data
4. Integration with existing systems
5. Deployment into operational workflows

---

### Target Audience Analysis

**Primary Decision Makers:**
- Business owners (собственники бизнеса)
- Commercial directors (коммерческие директора)
- Head of Sales (руководители отделов продаж)
- Development directors (директора по развитию)
- Mid-to-large business executives

**Psychological Profile:**
- Time-constrained professionals
- Scan content diagonally, not linear reading
- Quickly assess competence through visual and textual signals
- Averse to marketing fluff and hype
- Demand concrete outcomes and clarity
- Accustomed to professional B2B communication standards
- Value precision over enthusiasm
- Trust is earned through restraint, not exaggeration

**Key Questions They Need Answered in Seconds:**
1. Who are you?
2. What exactly do you do?
3. Is this for someone like me?
4. What should I do next?

---

### Pricing Segment Implications

High-ticket B2B consulting (100,000+ RUB) requires:

- **Visual maturity:** No startup aesthetics, no experimental UI
- **Authority signals:** Methodology, process clarity, professional documentation
- **Trust architecture:** Legal compliance, clear terms, professional presentation
- **Restraint:** Confidence doesn't need to shout
- **Editorial quality:** Typography and spacing as quality indicators

---

### Conversion Goal

**Primary Action:** Lead form submission ("Обсудить задачу")

**Secondary Action:** Document review (presentation, report example)

**Conversion Path:**
Hero → Understand value proposition → Review process credibility → Optional document validation → Form submission

## PHASE 2: THREE VISUAL CONCEPTS

---

### CONCEPT 1: "ARCHITECTURAL PRECISION"

#### Visual Idea
Inspired by architectural blueprints and technical documentation. The design treats information as structured data, using grid systems, precise alignment, and monospaced accents to convey engineering-level accuracy.

#### Color Palette
- **Background:** #FAFAFA (off-white, paper-like)
- **Surface:** #FFFFFF (pure white cards)
- **Primary Text:** #1A1A1A (near-black)
- **Secondary Text:** #525252 (medium gray)
- **Accent:** #0066CC (classic blue, corporate trust)
- **Border:** #E5E5E5 (subtle dividers)
- **Muted:** #737373 (tertiary text)

#### Typography
- **Primary Font:** Inter (clean, modern, highly readable)
- **Monospace Accent:** JetBrains Mono or IBM Plex Mono (for numbers, labels, technical elements)
- **Hierarchy:** Strong size contrast, generous line-height for readability

#### Layout
- Strict 12-column grid
- Visible structural lines (subtle borders between sections)
- Asymmetric hero composition with clear focal point
- Process section as horizontal timeline with numbered stages
- Generous whitespace as luxury signal

#### Hero Section
- Left-aligned text block (eyebrow, H1, subtitle)
- Right side: abstract process diagram (SVG, minimal lines showing data flow)
- No photography, no illustrations
- CTA buttons with sharp corners (2px radius max)

#### Process Section
- Horizontal progression with connecting line
- Each stage in defined grid cell
- Numbering as large typographic element (01, 02, 03)
- Results highlighted in separate visual container
- Divider lines between stages

#### Form Design
- Single-column layout
- Input fields with bottom border only (no full rectangle)
- Labels above fields in small caps
- Checkbox with custom styled box
- Submit button: solid accent color, no gradient

#### Footer
- Two-column: contacts left, legal right
- Thin top border separator
- Minimal styling, maximum clarity

#### Responsive Behavior
- Mobile: stacked layout, grid becomes single column
- Tablet: hybrid approach, process becomes vertical
- Desktop: full grid expression

#### Advantages
- Communicates precision and methodology
- High trust through architectural references
- Easy to scan for busy executives
- Distinguishes from typical SaaS landing pages
- Scalable system for future pages

#### Disadvantages
- Risk of appearing too cold/technical
- Requires careful balance to remain approachable
- Monospace accents must be used sparingly

---

### CONCEPT 2: "EDITORIAL AUTHORITY"

#### Visual Idea
Inspired by high-end business publications (Harvard Business Review, McKinsey Quarterly). Treats the landing page as a magazine spread—typography-driven, content-first, with sophisticated hierarchy and editorial rhythm.

#### Color Palette
- **Background:** #FFFFFF (pure white, like premium paper)
- **Surface:** #F8F9FA (very light gray for subtle zones)
- **Primary Text:** #0D0D0D (deep black)
- **Secondary Text:** #404040 (charcoal)
- **Accent:** #C41E3A (deep red, authority and action) OR #1A4D2E (forest green, stability)
- **Border:** #EAEAEA (barely visible)
- **Muted:** #666666

#### Typography
- **Display/Hero:** Playfair Display or similar serif (for H1 only, adds gravitas)
- **Body/UI:** Inter or Suisse Int'l (clean sans-serif for everything else)
- **Eyebrow/Labels:** Small caps sans-serif with tracking
- **Hierarchy:** Dramatic size contrast, tight leading for headlines, loose for body

#### Layout
- Magazine-style asymmetric grid
- Large type as visual element
- Strategic use of negative space
- Section breaks with generous padding
- Content density varies for rhythm

#### Hero Section
- Centered or slightly off-center composition
- Eyebrow in small caps with letter-spacing
- H1 in serif font, large scale (56-72px desktop)
- Subtitle in clean sans-serif
- Dual CTA: primary solid, secondary outlined
- No illustration, typography carries visual weight

#### Process Section
- Vertical layout with large section numbers
- Each stage introduced with bold heading
- Bullet points in clean list format
- Result boxes with subtle background tint
- Thin horizontal rules between stages

#### Form Design
- Two-column layout on desktop
- Input fields with full border, subtle radius (4px)
- Floating labels or traditional labels above
- Clear error states with inline messages
- Submit button with hover state

#### Footer
- Centered logo/brand
- Three-column: contacts, documents, legal
- Policy links clearly visible
- Copyright minimal

#### Responsive Behavior
- Mobile: all centered, simplified hierarchy
- Tablet: maintains editorial feel with adjusted proportions
- Desktop: full magazine layout expression

#### Advantages
- Instantly signals seniority and expertise
- Familiar format for executive audience
- Typography quality = perceived company quality
- Timeless, won't look dated in 2 years
- Excellent readability

#### Disadvantages
- Serif font may feel too traditional for "tech" company
- Requires high-quality font licensing for best effect
- Less "technological" visual signaling

---

### CONCEPT 3: "TECHNICAL MINIMALISM"

#### Visual Idea
Inspired by enterprise software interfaces and developer tools (Vercel, Stripe Docs, Linear). Dark-mode optional, but primarily light theme with extreme reduction—only essential elements, everything else removed. Technology shown through structure, not decoration.

#### Color Palette
- **Background:** #0A0A0A (near-black) OR #FFFFFF (pure white—recommend light for B2B trust)
- **Surface:** #141414 (slightly lighter than bg) OR #FAFAFA
- **Primary Text:** #EDEDED (light text on dark) OR #171717 (dark on light)
- **Secondary Text:** #A3A3A3 OR #525252
- **Accent:** #3B82F6 (bright blue, tech standard) OR #0066FF
- **Border:** #262626 OR #E5E5E5
- **Success:** #10B981
- **Error:** #EF4444

**Recommendation:** Light theme for B2B trust, dark mode feels too "developer tool"

#### Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter exclusively (one font, multiple weights)
- **Weights:** 400 (body), 500 (medium), 600 (semi-bold), 700 (bold)
- **Sizes:** Modular scale (14, 16, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36, 48, 64)
- **Line Height:** Tight for headings (1.1-1.2), comfortable for body (1.6)

#### Layout
- Max-width 1200px content container
- Consistent side margins (desktop: 80-120px)
- Sections separated by 120-160px vertical spacing
- Grid-based component alignment
- No decorative backgrounds or patterns

#### Hero Section
- Split layout: text left (60%), visual right (40%)
- Visual: abstract workflow diagram (CSS/SVG only)
  - Shows: input → processing → output
  - Or: CRM connection visualization
  - Minimalist nodes and connections
- Eyebrow: labeled pill or simple text
- H1: 48-56px, 600 weight
- Subtitle: 18-20px, muted color
- CTAs: primary filled, secondary ghost button

#### Process Section
- Three equal-width columns on desktop
- Each card has: number badge, title, subtitle, bullets, result box
- Number badges: circular or squared, accent background
- Cards: no shadow, subtle border or background tint
- Result boxes: distinct background, icon or checkmark
- Horizontal connector line between stages (optional)

#### Form Design
- Contained in dedicated section with subtle background
- Input fields: full border, 6px radius, clear focus state
- Labels: 14px, medium weight, above inputs
- Error handling: red border + message below field
- Success state: confirmation message, form hidden
- Submit button: full-width on mobile, auto on desktop

#### Footer
- Simple two-row layout
- Top row: contacts (phone, telegram, email)
- Bottom row: legal info, policy links, copyright
- Minimal visual treatment

#### Responsive Behavior
- Mobile (<768px): single column, stacked sections
- Tablet (768-1024px): two-column process, adjusted spacing
- Desktop (1024px+): full layout expression

#### Advantages
- Clean, modern, professional
- Fast loading (minimal assets)
- Easy to maintain and extend
- Familiar pattern for tech-savvy buyers
- Scales well across devices

#### Disadvantages
- Risk of looking like generic SaaS template
- Less distinctive than other concepts
- May not communicate "high-ticket consulting" strongly enough

## PHASE 3: CONCEPT SELECTION

### Selected Concept: **CONCEPT 1 — "ARCHITECTURAL PRECISION"**

### Rationale

#### 1. B2B Alignment
Architectural precision directly communicates **methodology** and **systematic approach**—exactly what high-ticket B2B buyers expect from a consulting partner. Unlike Concept 2 (editorial) which leans toward thought leadership, or Concept 3 (technical minimalism) which risks generic SaaS vibes, Concept 1 positions i-pro.tech as an **implementation partner**, not just advisors.

#### 2. Trust Architecture
The blueprint/technical drawing aesthetic subconsciously signals:
- We plan before we execute
- We document our process
- We engineer solutions, not improvise
- Precision matters to us

This is critical for a service where clients are entrusting their sales operations (revenue-critical) to an AI system they don't fully understand.

#### 3. Price Point Justification
High-ticket services require visual signals that justify the investment. Architectural design language is associated with:
- Professional services (architecture firms charge premium rates)
- Engineering (precision = value)
- Custom solutions (not templates)

Concept 2 (editorial) could work but might feel more like a publication than a service provider. Concept 3 (minimalism) is too common in the SaaS world and doesn't differentiate at the premium end.

#### 4. Target Audience Resonance
Business owners and commercial directors are accustomed to:
- Technical specifications
- Process documentation
- Structured proposals
- Clear deliverables

The architectural metaphor speaks their language. They've seen blueprints, Gantt charts, process maps. This visual vocabulary is familiar and credible.

#### 5. Differentiation from AI Startups
Most AI companies lean into:
- Purple/blue gradients (Concept 3 risk)
- Abstract neural network visuals
- Futuristic aesthetics
- "Revolutionary" messaging

Concept 1 deliberately avoids these tropes. By using architectural/technical design language, i-pro.tech positions itself as:
- Grounded in reality
- Focused on implementation, not hype
- A consultant, not a vendor

#### 6. Readability & Scannability
The grid-based, structured layout supports diagonal reading:
- Clear section breaks
- Obvious hierarchy
- Numbered stages guide the eye
- Whitespace creates breathing room

Busy executives can extract key information in seconds without feeling overwhelmed.

#### 7. Visual Maturity
Architectural design has timeless qualities:
- Not trendy (won't look dated in 18 months)
- Professional without being stuffy
- Modern without being experimental
- Confident without being aggressive

This matches the desired positioning: serious technology consulting company.

#### 8. Scalability
The design system built on grid, typography, and subtle structural elements can easily extend to:
- Additional landing pages
- Case study templates
- Documentation pages
- Client portals

Concept 2 requires more bespoke layouts. Concept 3 is flexible but less distinctive.

---

### Why NOT Concept 2 (Editorial Authority)?

While editorial design is sophisticated and trustworthy:
- Serif fonts may feel too "traditional media" for a tech implementation company
- Less visual signaling of technical capability
- Better suited for thought leadership/content marketing than service sales
- May create distance between company and "hands-on implementation" perception

**Verdict:** Excellent for a consultancy focused on strategy/advice, less optimal for implementation-focused AI integration.

---

### Why NOT Concept 3 (Technical Minimalism)?

While clean and modern:
- Too common in the SaaS/startup world
- Risks blending in with lower-priced competitors
- Dark theme options feel too "developer tool"
- Doesn't sufficiently signal premium positioning
- Lacks the methodological storytelling of Concept 1

**Verdict:** Good for product-led growth, less effective for high-touch B2B consulting sales.
